# Harshith

Harshith is a curious, project-driven builder who enjoys turning ideas into working systems. He has a strong interest in technology, but his personality extends well beyond just coding.

Harshith enjoys:

- **Building with technology**: Loves coding, AI/ML, systems, automation, and experimenting with new frameworks, tools, and hardware. He learns best by actually building things rather than just studying theory.
- **AI & intelligent systems**: Particularly interested in local-first AI, agents, RAG, memory systems, computer vision, and creating practical assistants that can interact with the real world.
- **Software & systems engineering**: Enjoys backend development, developer tooling, Linux, self-hosting, Docker, databases, and understanding how things work beneath the surface.
- **Hardware & experimentation**: Likes working with GPUs, embedded systems, Raspberry Pi, Jetson, ESP boards, and computers—not just using technology, but tinkering with it.
- **Hackathons & ambitious projects**: Enjoys taking on difficult problems, exploring unconventional ideas, and turning rough concepts into functional prototypes.
- **Entrepreneurship & community building**: Interested in creating products, organizing technical communities, collaborating with other builders, and turning technical ideas into something useful.
- **Cars & engineering**: Has an interest in cars and the engineering, technology, and design behind them.
- **Karate & discipline**: Values martial arts, discipline, consistency, leadership, and the sense of community that comes from practicing and teaching together.
- **Family & personal connections**: Values spending time with family and maintaining meaningful relationships outside of technology.
- **Sleep & downtime**: Appreciates good sleep and downtime as a counterbalance to his tendency to stay deeply engaged with projects.
- **Exploration & learning**: Enjoys going down technical rabbit holes, learning unfamiliar technologies, questioning how things work, and connecting ideas across different fields.

### How Harshith tends to work

Harshith is naturally curious and likes breadth as much as depth. He is willing to jump between AI, web development, infrastructure, hardware, entrepreneurship, and completely new domains when a problem interests him. He prefers **building over passively consuming**, practical experimentation over excessive theory, and lightweight solutions over unnecessary complexity.

His biggest strength is the ability to explore an idea from multiple angles and quickly turn it into something tangible. His biggest challenge is the other side of that curiosity: having many interesting ideas at once can lead to context switching and unfinished projects.

Overall, Harshith's interests reflect a balance between **building, experimenting, learning, competing, creating, staying disciplined, spending time with people he cares about, and occasionally doing absolutely nothing and sleeping.**
